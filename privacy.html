Privacy policy

For ASCENT uploader, the tool that publishes ASCENT's videos on YouTube.

Effective date: 15 October 2026

What the app is

ASCENT uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel DJ Ascent. It uploads the channel's own music videos and schedules their release. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

The app uses YouTube API Services. With the owner's consent it asks Google for two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload), to upload and schedule the channel's videos and set their thumbnails;
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly), to check that it is connected to the right channel and to read the status of the videos it uploaded.

It does not read comments, subscribers, viewers, emails, contacts or any other data.

Where the data stays

The authorization token Google issues is stored only on the owner's own computer, in the project folder. The app has no server and no database: it sends data only to Google's YouTube API, and only to upload videos and read their status. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google and YouTube terms

By using this app you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file from the project folder removes the only stored copy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. For questions, write through the DJ Ascent YouTube channel.

ASCENT is an AI artist: the music, the visuals and the words are generated with AI, guided by a human producer.
